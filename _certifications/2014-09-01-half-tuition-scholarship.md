This scholarship was awarded prior to starting the Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering program at Near East University, recognizing academic potential and merit.

## Award Overview

- **Award**: Half-Tuition Scholarship
- **Institution**: Near East University, Nicosia, Cyprus
- **Date Awarded**: September 2014
- **Duration**: Covers the entire bachelor's degree program (Sep 2014 – Jun 2018)

## Significance

This scholarship was granted based on:
- Academic performance and potential
- Merit-based selection criteria
- Commitment to pursuing higher education in Computer Engineering

## Impact

The scholarship provided:
- 50% reduction in tuition fees for the entire bachelor's program
- Financial support to focus on academic excellence
- Recognition of academic achievements prior to university enrollment

This award reflects a strong academic foundation and dedication to educational excellence from the outset of the university journey.
