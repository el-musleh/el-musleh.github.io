These certificates of honor were awarded during the Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering program at Near East University, recognizing consistent academic excellence across multiple semesters.

## Award Overview

- **Award**: Certificates of Honor
- **Institution**: Near East University, Nicosia, Cyprus
- **Period**: Sep 2014 – Jun 2018 (Bachelor's program)
- **Total Certificates**: 4

## Academic Achievements

The certificates were awarded for maintaining high GPAs throughout the bachelor's program:

### High Honour Certificates
- **GPA 3.64/4.0** - Awarded for exceptional academic performance
- **GPA 3.52/4.0** - Awarded for outstanding academic achievement

### Honour Certificates
- **GPA 3.30/4.0** - Awarded for academic excellence
- **GPA 3.22/4.0** - Awarded for strong academic performance

## Significance

These awards demonstrate:
- Consistent academic excellence across all semesters
- Strong dedication to maintaining high standards
- Ability to perform at a high level throughout the entire program
- Commitment to academic rigor and continuous improvement

## Impact

The certificates of honor represent:
- Recognition by the university for sustained academic achievement
- Validation of hard work and dedication to studies
- Foundation for continued professional and academic growth
- Evidence of strong work ethic and discipline

These awards complement the half-tuition scholarship received at the start of the program, showing a trajectory of academic excellence from beginning to graduation.
