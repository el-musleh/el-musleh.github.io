This award recognizes outstanding achievement in the Black Forest Hackathon 2026, where our team (Dogs in Wonderland) secured 3rd place out of 14 teams by developing an innovative solution addressing SmartRegion challenges. The 48-hour sprint brought together talented developers to create impactful solutions for regional innovation.

## Achievement Overview

- **Competition**: Black Forest Hackathon 2026
- **Organizer**: BadenCampus and Black Forest Innovation GmbH
- **Location**: Offenburg, Germany
- **Result**: 3rd place winner (out of 14 teams)
- **Team**: Dogs in Wonderland
- **Format**: 48-hour hackathon sprint
- **Theme**: SmartRegion

## Challenge Context

### Event Organization
- **Hosts**: BadenCampus and Black Forest Innovation GmbH
- **Sponsors**: Volksbank eG – Die Gestalterbank and Corewire IT Consulting
- **Challenge Partners**: Badenova, Badenova Netze, WeberHaus, and the Regierungspräsidium Freiburg

### Mentorship and Support
The achievement was made possible through exceptional mentorship from:
- **Laura Engist** (Nagarro)
- **Jerome Sommerfeldt** (Nagarro)

Their guidance was instrumental in helping architect and implement the solution within the 48-hour sprint timeframe.
