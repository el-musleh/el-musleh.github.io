This award recognizes outstanding achievement in the Carlsberg Hackathon 2020, where our team developed an innovative solution addressing key challenges in the brewing industry. The competition brought together talented developers and innovators to create impactful solutions.

## Achievement Overview

- **Competition**: Carlsberg Hackathon 2020
- **Organizer**: Carlsberg Group, Denmark
- **Format**: Online hackathon
- **Result**: First place winner
- **Team Size**: Cross-functional team of developers and domain experts

## Project Focus

Our winning solution addressed:
- Supply chain optimization challenges
- Digital transformation opportunities
- Sustainability initiatives
- Customer experience enhancement
- Operational efficiency improvements

## Skills Demonstrated

- **Rapid Prototyping**: Developing functional prototypes under time constraints
- **Team Collaboration**: Working effectively in a distributed team environment
- **Problem Analysis**: Understanding complex business requirements
- **Technical Innovation**: Applying cutting-edge technologies to solve real-world problems
- **Presentation Skills**: Effectively communicating solution value proposition

## Impact

The solution received recognition for:
- Innovation in traditional industry processes
- Practical applicability and implementation potential
- Strong business case and ROI projections
- Technical excellence and scalability
