This intensive workshop provided comprehensive training in 3D printing technologies, covering both theoretical foundations and hands-on practical experience. The workshop was conducted at the Angstrom Makerspace, providing access to professional-grade 3D printing equipment.

## Workshop Overview

- **Organizer**: Engineer to Engineer (E2E) Uppsala
- **Location**: Angstrom Makerspace (Onsite)
- **Duration**: Full-day intensive workshop
- **Format**: Theoretical instruction + hands-on practice

## Topics Covered

### 3D Printing Technologies
- FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling) principles
- SLA (Stereolithography) overview
- SLS (Selective Laser Sintering) introduction
- Material selection and properties

### Design for 3D Printing
- CAD software fundamentals
- Design optimization for additive manufacturing
- Support structures and orientation
- Tolerance and fit considerations

### Practical Applications
- Prototyping workflows
- Functional part design
- Post-processing techniques
- Quality assessment methods

## Hands-On Experience

Participants gained practical experience in:
- Operating 3D printers
- Preparing 3D models for printing
- Troubleshooting common issues
- Optimizing print settings

## Skills Acquired

- **Technical Skills**: 3D printer operation, model preparation, troubleshooting
- **Design Skills**: Design thinking for additive manufacturing, optimization techniques
- **Problem Solving**: Identifying and resolving printing issues
- **Project Management**: From concept to physical prototype
